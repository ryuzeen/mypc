# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, hosted on GitHub Pages (`ryuzeen.github.io/mypc`). All images and fonts live in the repo; nothing hotlinked from stores.

## Users

- Steam visitors who click the link on ryu's profile, curious to see the setup. Quick glance, mostly on desktop, some on phone.
- Friends and visitors ryu wants to show the setup off to.
- People planning a strong all-round gaming PC who want a working reference: parts, specs, typical price, where to look.

## Product Purpose

A personal page showing ryu's current PC and desk peripherals. Success: a visitor gets the whole setup in seconds, it looks good enough to be worth sharing, and someone building a PC can read the parts, specs, price and expected performance.

## Positioning

It is one specific real setup, not a store or a build guide. Everything on it is ryu's actual hardware.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Name: only the nick "ryu". No "Ryuzeen", no "Lab", no pronouns referring to ryu.
- No avatar/profile photo (removed by request).
- Copy in PT-BR, plain and direct; no AI-sounding marketing lines.
- Prices are an average of current Brazilian market listings, labelled as approximate, with research date.
- Every item (PC parts and peripherals) links to a Google search for that product.
- Performance data must be real: either measured on this PC or clearly labelled as typical numbers from public reviews of the same CPU+GPU, with sources. Never invented.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product cut-out images: `assets/img/*.webp` (manufacturer/store photos, backgrounds removed).
- Parts list: RTX 4070 Super (Gigabyte Windforce OC), Ryzen 7 7700X, Kingston Fury Beast 32 GB DDR5 5200, ASUS TUF B650M-Plus WiFi, MSI MAG A850GL, Kingston NV2 1 TB, Liketec Madness Cube.
- Peripherals: AOC Agon 240 Hz 1080p, WLmouse Beast X Max, Logitech G Pro X TKL Rapid, Logitech G Pro X 2 Lightspeed, Kanji mousepad 45×40.
- No measured benchmarks from this PC yet.

## Product Principles

1. The hardware is the hero; interface recedes behind it.
2. Truthful numbers only: prices dated and approximate, performance sourced.
3. Glanceable first, detailed on demand.
4. Feels crafted, not generated.
